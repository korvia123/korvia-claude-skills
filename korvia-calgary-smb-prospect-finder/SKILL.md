# CREATE A CLAUDE SKILL: KORVIA CALGARY SMB PROSPECT FINDER

Create a Claude Skill called:

**Korvia Calgary SMB Prospect Finder**

## 1. PURPOSE

This skill finds and researches high-potential prospects for **Korvia AI Consulting** in Calgary and surrounding communities.

The target is:

* Small businesses
* SMEs
* Solopreneurs
* Business owners
* Owner-operated companies
* Local professional practices
* Small multi-location businesses

The skill's purpose is NOT to generate a huge list of companies.

Its purpose is to identify businesses where there is a **credible, practical, and potentially valuable AI opportunity**.

The output will be passed to a separate skill:

**Korvia Personalized Outreach & Targeted Marketing**

That second skill will use the intelligence generated here to create personalized outreach.

---

# 2. GEOGRAPHIC FOCUS

Primary market:

**Calgary, Alberta**

Also search surrounding communities when relevant:

* Airdrie
* Cochrane

# 3. TARGET BUSINESS PROFILE

Look primarily for businesses that have one or more of these characteristics:

* Regular customer inquiries
* Meaningful inbound phone calls
* Appointment scheduling
* Lead generation
* Customer service workload
* Repetitive administrative work
* Manual follow-up
* Repetitive customer questions
* Quoting or estimate processes
* Dispatching or scheduling
* Customer intake
* Multiple employees handling repetitive tasks
* Growing customer demand
* Owner overwhelmed by administrative work
* Small teams doing work that could potentially be automated
* High-value leads
* After-hours inquiries
* Missed-call risk
* Poor or slow customer response
* Heavy dependence on the owner

Prioritize businesses where solving one of these problems could have a meaningful business impact.

* New services

* Owner-operated business

# 8. PROSPECT SCORE

Score prospects out of 100 using:

### Business Fit — 20 points

Is this a strong fit for Korvia's SMB focus?

### AI Opportunity — 25 points

Is there a clear practical AI use case?

### Pain Signal — 20 points

Is there evidence of a relevant business problem?

### Buying Signal — 15 points

Is there evidence of growth, change, hiring, or technology investment?

### Economic Potential — 10 points

Could solving the problem create meaningful value?

### Decision-Maker Accessibility — 10 points

Can we reasonably identify/contact the owner or relevant decision-maker?

Total:

**__/100**

Always explain the score briefly.

This score represents **AI opportunity and outreach priority**, NOT probability of purchase.

---

# 9. PROSPECT TIERS

Use:

### A — High-Potential

Strong evidence of a meaningful AI opportunity.

### B — Potential

Good fit but weaker evidence.

### C — Low Priority

Possible fit but insufficient evidence or weak opportunity.

Only provide detailed outreach intelligence for A and strong B prospects.

# 12. SEARCH STRATEGY

When searching for prospects, do NOT use only:

> “Calgary plumbers”

Instead combine:

**Industry + business signal + location**

Examples:

* Calgary accounting firms hiring administrative staff
* Calgary dental clinics online booking
* Calgary property managers tenant inquiries
* Calgary contractors quote requests
* Calgary law firms consultation requests
* Calgary businesses hiring receptionists
* Calgary businesses hiring customer service representatives
* Calgary companies expanding locations
* Calgary businesses with heavy phone dependence

Look for businesses where the underlying problem may create an AI opportunity.

---

# 13. OWNER-FOCUSED PROSPECTING

For small businesses and solopreneurs, prioritize the owner.

Look for signals such as:

* Owner is involved in day-to-day operations
* Owner handles customer communication
* Owner handles sales
* Owner handles scheduling
* Owner is hiring support
* Business appears dependent on the owner's time

These businesses may respond particularly well to messaging focused on:

**“How much of your time is being consumed by repetitive work that AI could handle?”**

Do not assume this is true; use it as an investigation angle.

# 18. FINAL DECISION TEST

Before including a prospect, ask:

### 1. Is this a real Calgary-area SMB?

### 2. Is there evidence of a potential business problem?

### 3. Can AI plausibly help solve it?

### 4. Could solving it create meaningful business value?

### 5. Can we identify who likely makes the decision?

### 6. Is there a specific reason Korvia should contact this business?

### 7. Can the Personalized Outreach Skill create a genuinely personalized message from the information gathered?

If most answers are YES, include the prospect.

If not, lower the priority or exclude it.
