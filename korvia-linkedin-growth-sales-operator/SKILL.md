# 17. LEAD QUALIFICATION

The skill must classify LinkedIn leads based on actual evidence.

### COLD

No meaningful engagement or business signal.

### ENGAGED

Interacted with Korvia content or responded positively.

### POTENTIAL

Shows a relevant business problem or AI interest.

### QUALIFIED

There is evidence of:

* Relevant business problem
* Appropriate company
* Decision-making authority or influence
* Potential AI opportunity
* Genuine interest

### SALES CONVERSATION

The prospect has expressed interest in discussing the problem or solution.

### MEETING

A meeting has been scheduled.

Never mark someone as qualified simply because they accepted a connection request.

---

# 18. LEAD QUALIFICATION QUESTIONS

Determine:

### BUSINESS FIT

Does the business fit Korvia's target market?

### PAIN

Is there a meaningful business problem?

### AI OPPORTUNITY

Could AI realistically help?

### AUTHORITY

Does the person influence the decision?

### TIMING

Is there evidence the issue matters now?

### INTEREST

Has the prospect demonstrated interest?

Use these factors to determine lead status.

# 19. RESPONSE HANDLING

When a prospect replies, analyze the message before drafting a response.

Classify the response as:

* Interested
* Curious
* Neutral
* Objection
* Not interested
* Already has solution
* Wants more information
* Wants pricing
* Wants demo
* Wants meeting
* Wrong person
* Referral
* Spam/irrelevant

Then recommend the appropriate response.

---

# 20. OBJECTION HANDLING

Common objections:

### “We already use AI.”

Determine what they use and identify whether there is another opportunity.

### “Not interested.”

Do not pressure them.

### “Send me information.”

Provide concise, relevant information and establish a natural next step.

### “How much does it cost?”

Do not invent pricing.

Explain that pricing depends on the workflow and scope, then move toward a discovery conversation where appropriate.

### “We don't need AI.”

Do not argue.

Ask whether there are operational or customer-service processes they would like to improve.

---

# 21. LEAD NURTURING

Not every prospect should be pushed toward a meeting immediately.

Create nurture paths for:

* Interested but not ready
* Curious but uncertain
* Budget constrained
* Existing solution
* Timing issue
* Needs internal approval
* Long-term prospect

Use relevant future content rather than repetitive sales messages.
