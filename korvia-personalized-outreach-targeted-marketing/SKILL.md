# CREATE A CLAUDE SKILL: KORVIA PERSONALIZED OUTREACH & TARGETED MARKETING

Create a Claude Skill called:

**Korvia Personalized Outreach & Targeted Marketing**

## 1. PURPOSE

This skill is responsible for turning prospect information into highly personalized, persuasive, and commercially focused marketing and outreach campaigns for **Korvia AI Consulting**.

Korvia AI Consulting helps small and medium-sized businesses in Calgary and Alberta identify and implement practical AI solutions that improve profitability, productivity, customer experience, and business growth.

I already have a separate **Prospect Finder Skill** that identifies businesses and prospects.

This skill should NOT primarily find prospects.

Instead, it should take prospect information provided by the Prospect Finder Skill or by me and determine:

* What matters to this business
* What problems they are likely experiencing
* Where AI could create measurable value
* What marketing angle is most relevant
* How Korvia should approach them
* What message should be sent
* Which channel should be used
* What follow-up sequence should be used
* How to move the prospect toward a conversation or demo

The goal is **qualified conversations and meetings**, not generic outreach volume.

# 4. CORE OPERATING PRINCIPLE

Every outreach message must answer:

**“Why should THIS business care?”**

Do not create generic messages such as:

> “Hi, we help businesses leverage AI to increase efficiency. Would you be interested in learning more?”

Instead, identify a specific business signal and build the message around it.

Examples of useful signals:

* Website claims 24/7 service
* Calls are important to the business
* Slow response to inquiries
* No obvious after-hours coverage
* Appointment-based business
* Heavy dependence on administrative work
* Repetitive customer questions
* Manual lead intake
* Poor or outdated website
* No online booking
* Multiple locations
* Growing business
* Hiring administrative/customer service staff
* High volume of reviews
* Complaints about response times
* Strong lead-generation activity
* Large service area
* Complex customer intake process
* Repetitive quoting or scheduling
* Existing CRM
* Existing automation
* Existing chatbot
* Existing AI tools
* Signs of operational inefficiency
* Business growth or expansion
* New service offerings
* High-value customer leads
* Missed-call risk
* Customer support bottlenecks

Use publicly available business information where appropriate.

* Who they serve
* Geographic market
* Business model

* Approximate business size when available

### B. Business Signals

### C. Likely Pain Points

Identify 1–3 likely operational or customer-acquisition problems.

### D. AI Opportunity

Identify the most relevant Korvia solution.

### E. Business Outcome

Explain what the solution could potentially improve:

* More captured leads
* Faster response
* More booked appointments
* Reduced administrative workload
* Better customer experience
* Reduced missed opportunities
* Improved operational efficiency
* Better follow-up
* Lower repetitive workload
* Increased scalability

Do not invent numerical ROI unless there is evidence to support it.

# 7. OUTREACH ANGLE SELECTION

Before writing the message, select the strongest angle.

Possible angles include:

### MISSED OPPORTUNITY ANGLE

Use when the business appears vulnerable to missed calls, slow response, or lost leads.

### REVENUE LEAK ANGLE

Focus on opportunities that may be lost because of slow follow-up, missed calls, or poor lead handling.

### TIME-SAVING ANGLE

Use when employees appear to spend significant time on repetitive administrative tasks.

### CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE ANGLE

Use when customers may benefit from faster responses, automated communication, or 24/7 assistance.

### GROWTH ANGLE

Use when the business appears to be growing and may need automation to scale.

### COMPETITIVE ADVANTAGE ANGLE

Use when AI adoption could differentiate the business.

### AFTER-HOURS ANGLE

Use for businesses that receive inquiries outside normal working hours.

### AI OPPORTUNITY ANGLE

Use when the business has obvious workflows that could benefit from AI but no visible automation.

### CUSTOM OPERATIONAL ANGLE

Use when a specific workflow is clearly identifiable.

Choose ONE primary angle unless there is a compelling reason to combine two.

# 10. STRONG HOOK REQUIREMENT

The first sentence should ideally reference something specific about the prospect.

Examples:

* A business practice
* Their website
* Their hours
* Their service model
* A customer experience issue
* A business expansion signal
* A specific operational opportunity

Weak:

> “I wanted to introduce you to Korvia AI Consulting.”

Better:

> “I noticed your website says you’re available 24/7, but there’s an opportunity to make sure calls are still handled when your team can’t answer.”

The message should create curiosity without making unsupported accusations.

The primary objective is to start a conversation.

* “Worth showing you what this could look like for [Company]?”
* “Would you be open to a quick call?”

**Free 30-minute AI strategy session**

# 19. LEAD PRIORITIZATION

When enough information is available, classify prospects based on outreach readiness:

**HOT**
Strong business signal + obvious AI opportunity + strong reason to contact now.

**WARM**
Good AI opportunity but weaker immediate buying signal.

**COLD**
Potential fit but limited evidence of an immediate need.

This is a prioritization system, NOT a claim about the prospect's likelihood to buy.

Explain the reasoning.

* Revenue
* Employee count
* Customer complaints
* Missed calls
* Technology
* Business problems
* Owner information
* Business events
* AI adoption
* Customer volume
* ROI
* Pricing
* Reviews

If information cannot be verified, clearly label it as an assumption or hypothesis.

---

# 21. CONTEXTUAL INTELLIGENCE

The skill should think beyond the obvious.

For example, if a business says:

**“Available 24/7”**

do not automatically say they need an AI receptionist.

Instead investigate:

* How customers currently contact them
* Whether calls are answered after hours
* Whether voicemail is used
* Whether online booking exists
* Whether urgent inquiries require immediate response
* Whether there are signs of slow response
* Whether an AI receptionist would actually solve a meaningful problem

The objective is to identify the **business case**, not force a Korvia product into every prospect.

---

# 22. CONVERSION OBJECTIVE

The ultimate objective is:

**Prospect → Conversation → Discovery → Personalized Demo → AI Strategy Session → Proposal → Client**

Do not try to sell everything in the first message.

The initial outreach should primarily earn the next conversation.

---

# 24. FINAL RULE

Before generating any outreach message, ask yourself:

**“If I were the owner receiving this message, would I immediately understand why this person contacted MY business specifically?”**

If the answer is no, improve the personalization.

The skill should optimize for:

**Relevance > Volume**

**Specificity > Generic AI messaging**

**Business outcomes > Technology features**

**Conversation > Hard sell**

**Evidence > Assumptions**

Build this as a reusable Claude Skill with clear instructions, decision logic, input/output structure, and reusable templates.
